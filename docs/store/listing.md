## Full description

Sideplay turns your Android TV or Google TV into a receiver for AirPlay, so your iPhone or iPad can show its screen or play videos on the big screen.

• Screen Mirroring: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, choose your TV.
• Videos: tap the AirPlay icon in apps such as YouTube, Photos and Safari, and the video plays on the TV while your phone stays free.
• Music: AirPlay audio plays on the TV with the album art and track title.
• Optional on-screen code, so only people in the room can connect.
• Starts with the TV, so it's ready whenever you are.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). No ads, no account, no tracking. Everything stays on your home network.

Your phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Some apps, such as Google Drive, send only audio over AirPlay; use Screen Mirroring for those.
